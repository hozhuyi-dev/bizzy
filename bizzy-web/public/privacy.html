Bizzy privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Bizzy is a private back-office tool used by a single physiotherapy practice to record sessions, keep client details and issue receipts. It is not offered to the public, and only the practice owner can sign in.

What Bizzy accesses

With the practice owner's permission, Bizzy:

  • reads events from one Google Calendar she chooses (read only);
  • reads, creates and updates contacts in her Google Contacts, and adds clients to a “Clients” label.

How it is used

Calendar events are turned into session records, so sessions can be priced and receipts issued. Contacts hold each client's name, phone, email and address, which appear in the app and on receipts. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored

Session and client records, and the authorization Google grants, are stored in the practice owner's own Amazon Web Services account. Data is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

Google user data

Bizzy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnect Google in Bizzy's Settings, or remove Bizzy at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Either stops all access to the calendar and contacts. Nothing in them is changed by disconnecting.
